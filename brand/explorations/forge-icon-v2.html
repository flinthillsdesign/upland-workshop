Forge — v2

Fresh Directions

v1 was 18 takes on "anvil with dots above." This set abandons that — different objects (hearth, crucible, ingot, horseshoe), shape ambiguity (anvil-as-mountain, anvil-as-chevron), and character (Forge the smith, à la Tony).

Each grouping below is a different concept, not a variation. Pick the concept that feels right; we'll iterate on whichever lands.
