ODIN — Icon Explorations

Mythical Concepts

Playing with Norse mythology motifs — Odin's ravens, his single eye, runes, Yggdrasil, Gungnir (his spear), and the valknut. All using the brand palette.

A — The All-Seeing Eye
Odin sacrificed an eye at Mimir's well for wisdom. A single open eye — knowledge, oversight, seeing everything. Fits ODIN's role as the admin/hub app.
Geometric Eye Clean almond shape
Scarred Eye Eye with sacrifice mark
All-Seeing Radiating wisdom
B — Huginn & Muninn (The Ravens)
Odin's two ravens fly across the world each day, bringing back information. Huginn (thought) and Muninn (memory). Perfect metaphor for a system that watches and reports.
Raven Flight Angular bird in flight
Twin Ravens Huginn & Muninn facing
Wing Spread Abstract feather rays
C — Runes & Ansuz
Odin discovered the runes by hanging from Yggdrasil for nine days. The Ansuz rune (ᚨ) is literally "Odin's rune" — meaning divine breath, communication, wisdom.
Ansuz Odin's own rune
Ansuz Refined With wisdom glow
Runic Seal Ansuz in protective circle
D — Gungnir (The Spear)
Odin's spear that never misses its mark. Clean, decisive, authoritative. Speaks to ODIN being the central command app.
Gungnir The never-miss spear
Spearhead Bold upward thrust
Gungnir Outline Minimal stroke
E — Valknut
Three interlocking triangles — one of Odin's most recognizable symbols. Represents interconnection, power over life and death. The interlocking nature fits ODIN connecting all the apps.
Valknut Three linked triangles
Valknut Layered Depth + dimension
F — Yggdrasil (World Tree)
The cosmic tree connecting all nine realms. Odin hung from it to gain wisdom. Could represent ODIN as the root connecting all satellite apps.
World Tree Minimal Yggdrasil
Network Tree Realms as nodes
G — Hybrid Concepts
Combining elements: eye + rune, raven + sparkle, spear + radiance.
Eye of Ansuz Rune as pupil
Raven + Sparkle Bridging old + new
Gungnir's Gaze Spear + all-seeing eye
